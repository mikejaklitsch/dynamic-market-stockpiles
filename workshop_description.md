[h1]Dynamic Market Stockpiles[/h1]

In 1.3.0, global goods prices crash because of the new scaling increase in supply from market stockpiles. The amount of supply this adds is completely disconnected from the size of local economies and doesn't scale as nations develop. Small markets get the same stockpile capacity as large ones, flooding the global supply and tanking prices across the board.

This mod dynamically scales market stockpile sizes based on the actual value of the market. 2500 market value is the baseline for vanilla stockpile capacity, with a minimum of 5% of vanilla values and no upper limit. The result is less supply sitting around in less developed markets, which pushes prices back towards more reasonable levels.

[h2]Burgher Trading (Game Rule)[/h2]

There's an optional game rule to disable burgher trading by hitting their capacity with a -1000000% modifier. It's off by default. This might help stop trade from spreading good surpluses around too quickly, but it's not needed for the mod to work. Just there if you want to try it.
